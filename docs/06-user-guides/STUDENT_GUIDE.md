# Musomi Manager — Student Portal Quick Guide

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the student portal address provided by your school.
2. Select your school on the login page.
3. Enter your admission number and your personal password.
4. Sign in.

If you cannot sign in, check the selected school and admission number. Ask the school administrator to verify that your account is active or set a new individual password. Do not ask another student to share their password.

## 2. View your dashboard

The dashboard shows information associated with your own student account. Check the class and term information and use the navigation links to open marks, results, reports, or your profile.

## 3. View marks and results

- Open **Marks** to see published assessment marks available to you.
- Open a subject to see its assessment details.
- Open **Results** to see the current results summary.
- A missing mark is not the same as zero. If a mark is missing or looks incorrect, contact your teacher or school office.

Only published marks are visible in the student portal.

## 4. Download a report card

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Find the report for the required term.
3. Select its download link.
4. Open the PDF and check that your name, class, term, subjects, and marks are correct.

If the expected report is not listed, contact the school office. Teachers or administrators must generate reports before they appear for download.

## 5. Update your password

Open your profile/account settings and use the password-change form. Enter your current password, choose a new password that meets the displayed rules, and confirm it. Keep the new password private.

## 6. Protect your account

- Do not share your password.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- Do not use another student's account.
- Tell the school if you see information that does not belong to you.
